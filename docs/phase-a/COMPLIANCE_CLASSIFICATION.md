# UAE / Abu Dhabi healthcare requirements — classification
Prepared 26 Oct 2026 for FMC Al Ain (Abu Dhabi emirate, regulator: Department of Health – Abu Dhabi, DoH).
**This is not legal advice and makes no compliance or certification claim.** The official DoH PDFs could not be opened from this environment (host not reachable), so statements below come from search results quoting DoH pages and from law-firm summaries. Each row says what evidence was seen. FMC's compliance officer/legal counsel must confirm against primary texts.

Classes: **VM** Verified Mandatory (stated by an official source seen in this research) · **RSP** Recommended Security Practice · **RLC** Requires Legal/Compliance Confirmation.

## What this means for the build (all subject to confirmation)
UAE-resident hosting and backups · 25-year retention-safe design (deactivate, never delete clinical data) · MFA for administrators · per-patient access logging · encryption in transit and at rest · tested incident/breach process · integration with Malaffi treated as a separate approved project.
