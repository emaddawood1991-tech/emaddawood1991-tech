Reception guide

Front-desk staff: register patients, check them in, book appointments, manage the queue.

Sign in. Open the trial address, type your username and password. If asked, choose a new password (10+ characters). Turn on two-step verification in My Account if the trial lead asks you to.
Trial username: receptionPassword (given by the trial lead):

Your day

  1. Visits → type 3+ letters, an EMR number or a phone number. Pick the patient, or press ＋ New patient (name, phone, date of birth).
  2. Choose the department (and service if known) → Check in. The patient gets a queue number.
  3. Queue: watch waiting times. Use Priority (with a reason) only for a real reason; the clinician calls the next patient.
  4. Patient leaves before being called? Queue → No-show, or Cancel with a short reason. Nothing is deleted.
  5. Daily Schedule: + Add appointment → patient, department, date and time. Red conflicts mean the slot is taken. Booked patients: Arrived → check-in.
  6. Typo in a name or phone? Visits → select the patient → Edit details. The EMR number never changes.
  7. Two records for one person? Duplicates → Report a suspected duplicate (both EMR numbers + why). A manager reviews it.
  8. After treatment ends: Visits → Check out.
Do
  • Search before creating a new patient.
  • Give the patient their queue number.
  • Write a clear reason for priority, cancel or edit.
  • Sign out when you leave the desk.
Don’t
  • Don’t share your password or sign in for someone else.
  • Don’t type real patient data in the trial.
  • Don’t book on a patient marked “linked” — use the record it points to.
If something goes wrong
  • Locked out or forgot your password: press “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page, then phone the trial lead.
  • A screen looks wrong or a button does nothing: note the screen and time on the feedback sheet.
