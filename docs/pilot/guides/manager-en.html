Clinic manager guide

Clinic managers: run the floor, approve corrections, review reports, review duplicate records, set breaks and blocks.

Sign in. Sign in. Two-step verification is required for managers: scan the code with an authenticator app and keep the ten recovery codes safe.
Trial username: superPassword (given by the trial lead):

Your day

  1. Dashboard / Queue / Rooms: see waiting, in treatment, rooms free, staff available. Room status: Cleaning, Maintenance (reason needed).
  2. Corrections: open each request, read the reason and the original time, Approve or Reject with a note. You cannot approve your own.
  3. Daily Schedule: add Blocks (break, leave, meeting). Booking over a block needs an override reason — only managers allowed to override can do it.
  4. Reports: choose dates and filters. People = each person once; Visits and Sessions count what happened. Export CSV is limited and watermarked.
  5. Duplicates: review pairs, choose the record to keep, write why → Propose. A different manager or the administrator must approve.
  6. Queue: set Priority with a reason; cancel or no-show with a reason.
  7. Emergency access uses: the administrator reviews them; you may be asked what happened.
Do
  • Check the original time before approving a correction.
  • Review a duplicate pair’s date of birth and sex carefully.
  • Keep your recovery codes in a safe place.
Don’t
  • Don’t approve a link you proposed — it will be refused.
  • Don’t use overrides to hide mistakes; every one is audited.
  • Don’t export data you don’t need.
If something goes wrong
  • Lost your phone: ask the administrator to reset your two-step verification.
  • Something in a report looks wrong: note the filters and time on the feedback sheet.
