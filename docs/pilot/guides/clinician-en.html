Clinician guide

Physiotherapists, doctors and other treating staff: call patients, run and document treatment.

Sign in. Sign in with your username and password. You see your own department’s patients only. Turn on two-step verification in My Account if asked.
Trial username: pt.onePassword (given by the trial lead):

Your day

  1. Set My status (bottom left) to Available.
  2. Queue → Call next. The patient is called; reception or you assign a room (and bed if the room has beds).
  3. Rooms or Visits → Assign room → Enter room when the patient arrives.
  4. Visits → Start treatment → choose the service you are authorized for. Set the planned minutes if different. The timer starts.
  5. Sessions → Documentation: fill the four required fields — complaint, diagnosis, procedure code, progress note. The session turns from YELLOW to ORANGE when all four are saved.
  6. At the planned time the system stops the treatment and checks the patient out by itself (unverified until you confirm). Pause or Complete early with the buttons if needed.
  7. Forgot to complete? Corrections → request a correction with a reason; a manager approves. The original time is kept.
  8. Real emergency, patient in another department? My Account → Emergency access (only if the administrator turned it on).
Do
  • Document while the details are fresh.
  • Pause when the patient leaves the bed.
  • Use only services you are authorized for.
  • Use emergency access only for a real emergency.
Don’t
  • Don’t treat under another person’s sign-in.
  • Don’t leave a session running when the patient has gone.
  • Don’t paste real patient data into the trial.
If something goes wrong
  • “Not authorized for this service”: ask the administrator to add it to your account.
  • A session ended too early or late: Corrections.
  • Locked out: “Forgot password?” then phone the trial lead.
