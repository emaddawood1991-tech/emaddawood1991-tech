# The three movements — what the reference shows (studied frame by frame) and how to produce them
The reference (117 s) shows exactly these three, in this order. Our version must be newly filmed/generated: different person, room, clothing, no reference footage.
## Movement 1 — prone lumbar extension (reference ≈ 34–60 s; side view, carpeted floor)
A. Lie face-down flat. B. Prop up on the forearms/elbows (chest lifts, hips and pelvis stay on the floor), hold gently. C. Progress, if symptoms allow, to straight-ish arms pressing up on the hands, pelvis and thighs still on the floor, glutes/lower back relaxed, slow; lower back down. Side camera, full body in frame.
**Flow/Veo prompt 1A (8 s):** Photorealistic, vertical 9:16, locked-off side view in a bright living room. An adult man in a plain grey t-shirt and black shorts lies face down on a yoga mat, then slowly raises his chest, propping himself on his forearms with elbows under shoulders, pelvis and legs resting on the mat, head neutral. Calm, controlled, natural movement. No text, no dialogue, no music.
**Flow/Veo prompt 1B (8 s):** Same man, same room and camera. From prone on forearms, he slowly pushes through his hands to straighten his arms partially, lifting his chest while his hips stay on the mat and his legs relaxed; he holds one second, then lowers slowly back down. Gentle, not maximal. No text, no dialogue, no music.
## Movement 2 — side-lying rotation (reference ≈ 64–89 s; sofa)
A. Sit then lie on one side (painful side up), knees and hips bent. B. Top leg bent/draped forward and supported, bottom arm extended; top arm opens out behind as the upper trunk rotates gently toward the ceiling. C. Hold briefly, then return to the starting side-lying position; later the top leg is extended/bent to the other side.
**Prompt 2 (8 s, wide shot):** Photorealistic, vertical 9:16, a treatment couch/sofa in a bright room, camera slightly above the foot of the couch. An adult woman in a plain t-shirt and leggings lies on her side with knees bent, then rests her top knee forward on a pillow while slowly opening her top arm behind her to rotate her upper trunk toward the ceiling; she holds, then returns smoothly. Gentle range, no forcing. No text, no dialogue, no music.
**Prompt 2b (6 s, close-up):** Same scene; close-up on pelvis and lower back from behind as the trunk rotates slowly and returns. No text, no dialogue, no music.
## Movement 3 — 90/90 hip mobility (reference ≈ 93–107 s; gym turf)
A. Sit in 90/90: front leg shin forward, rear leg shin to the side, both knees ≈ 90°, torso tall. B. Keep the torso still while lifting the rear ankle off the floor and lowering it slowly (rotation at the hip; repeated). C. Slow forward lean of the trunk over the front leg (hands forward), then return to upright.
**Prompt 3A (8 s, front three-quarter):** Photorealistic, vertical 9:16, bright training studio with a green turf mat. An adult man in a grey t-shirt and shorts sits on the floor in a 90/90 hip position (front knee bent 90°, rear knee bent 90°), torso upright. Keeping his torso still, he slowly lifts his rear ankle off the floor and lowers it. Smooth, controlled. All joints and feet in frame. No text, no dialogue, no music.
**Prompt 3B (8 s):** Same person and room; in the 90/90 position he slowly leans his trunk forward over the front leg with hands reaching forward, pauses, then returns upright. No text, no dialogue, no music.
**Side-angle inserts (optional, 5 s each):** same movements from the side.
## Rules while producing
Same person and room across A/B of a movement; do not exaggerate range; reject clips with distorted hands/joints or text. Have a physiotherapist review each clip before publication (none of this is clinically approved yet).
