# Medical content guidelines

## Approved FMC services (only these may be offered as FMC services)
1. Physiotherapy — العلاج الطبيعي
2. Orthopedics — طب العظام
3. General Medicine — الطب العام
4. Neurological Rehabilitation — التأهيل العصبي
5. Sports Injury Treatment — علاج الإصابات الرياضية
6. Postoperative Rehabilitation — إعادة التأهيل بعد العمليات الجراحية

Do not invent departments, devices, procedures (e.g. injections, surgery, imaging, laser) or specialists. A post may *discuss* a condition without claiming FMC treats it with a specific modality. Pattern: «العلاج الطبيعي من خدمات مركز الفيصل الطبي، وتُحدَّد الخطة بعد التقييم».

## Claims policy
- Hedge: قد، غالبًا، في بعض الحالات، بحسب الحالة. No "يعالج نهائيًا", "شفاء مضمون", before/after promises, success rates, statistics (unless the user supplies a source).
- Don't imply every condition can be cured; don't imply physiotherapy suffices for every case.
- Never give a diagnosis; include "التشخيص بعد الفحص الطبي" where symptoms overlap. Add red-flag guidance (when to see a doctor) on symptom posts.
- Standard disclaimer (footer on single/last slides): «محتوى توعوي ولا يغني عن استشارة الطبيب».
- Awareness days: factual, respectful, no medical promises; mention only confirmed services.
- Flag anything uncertain to the user instead of guessing.

## Anatomical visuals
Must be anatomically plausible and labelled with HTML overlays. If a drawing is stylised, keep it schematic and correct (e.g. median nerve supplies thumb, index, middle and radial half of ring finger; little finger spared). Never claim an image is AI-generated unless a tool produced it; no unlicensed images.
