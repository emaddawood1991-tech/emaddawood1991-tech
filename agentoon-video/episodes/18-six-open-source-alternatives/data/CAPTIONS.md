## Instagram
Before you pay for another AI subscription, check these 6 open-source alternatives 👇
1) Open Generative AI — image & video studio (cloud models need a MuAPI key; local models don't)
2) OpenSEO — Semrush/Ahrefs alternative (bring your own DataForSEO key)
3) AppFlowy — Notion-style workspace (AGPL)
4) OpenWhispr — voice dictation, local or cloud models
5) Cal.diy — self-hosted scheduling (personal, non-production use per its README)
6) Cap — Loom-style screen recording
Open source ≠ always free: hosting, infrastructure and AI services can still cost money. Which would you try first? Save this + follow @agentoon.ai
