Overview
Claude Fable 5.2 is built for demanding reasoning and long-horizon agentic work. Typical uses include agent sessions that run for hours, multistep deep research, and analysis carried through to a finished document, spreadsheet, or deck.
Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2
Claude Mythos 5.2
offers the same capabilities only to organizations verified through Anthropic’s verification programs, such as the
Cyber Verification Program
. It shares Claude Fable 5.2’s specifications and pricing. To request access, apply to the program that covers your use case, or contact your account team.
