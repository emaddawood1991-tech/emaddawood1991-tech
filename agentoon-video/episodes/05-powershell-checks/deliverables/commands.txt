THE THREE COMMANDS (all read-only; copy exactly)

1) DNS servers
Get-DnsClientServerAddress -AddressFamily IPv4 | Select-Object InterfaceAlias,ServerAddresses

2) BitLocker (usually needs PowerShell opened as Administrator)
Get-BitLockerVolume | Select-Object MountPoint,ProtectionStatus,VolumeStatus

3) Latest hotfixes
Get-HotFix | Sort-Object InstalledOn -Descending | Select-Object -First 10 HotFixID,Description,InstalledOn

Notes
- Unfamiliar DNS addresses are worth checking (your router, ISP, or a public DNS you chose are normal). They do not prove anything is wrong.
- ProtectionStatus / VolumeStatus are what Windows reports for each volume; read them as-is.
- Get-HotFix lists installed hotfixes Windows reports, not the full Windows Update history.
