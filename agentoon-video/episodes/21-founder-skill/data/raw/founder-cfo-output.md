**The inputs are the plugin's example, not your numbers.** Replace them with real quotes before using any figure here.

- **The margin**: 38% profit at plan volume (383 cups a day). Contribution is $4.76 a cup (73%). Year 1 averages only 17% ($93,926 on $563,160), because months 1 to 7 run below plan volume.
- **The line to watch**: volume. Cutting volume 20% drops year 1 profit from $93,926 to $11,445 (-88%). Price -10% drops it to $37,610. Month 1 (140 a day) is below break-even (186 a day), so the first two months lose money. The ramp is the assumption to test first. No buyer panel results exist in `founder/`, so the ramp is unchecked.
- **Cash**: about $34,092 before the business pays for itself (the cumulative low, end of month 2). The startup money is earned back in month 8.
- **Three ways to improve the margin**:
  1. Price $6.50 to $7.00 (+$0.50): margin 38% to 42%, break-even 186 to 169 a day, year 1 profit $93,926 to $137,248.
  2. Price to $7.50 (+$1.00): margin 46%, year 1 profit $180,566, cash need $27,628. This assumes volume holds at a higher price, which is untested.
  3. Keep unit costs flat. Unit costs +15% cuts year 1 profit to $71,313, so supplier and card-fee prices are worth a quote. I did not run a unit-cost reduction what-if, so no figure is given for it.
- **Board conditions** (`founder/board.md`): that file does not exist, so they are not checked.

Revenue is before sales tax. Check whether your market adds sales tax on top.
